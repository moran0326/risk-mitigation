Public restrooms are a
silent risk
most people never think about.

We don't fail at hygiene because we don't care.
We fail because the environment doesn't support it. Our collection tackles the root: unconscious behavior in spaces where design currently works against us.

What the
science says
about restroom risk.

1.5m
Aerosol Height
Toilet plume reaches 1.5 meters within 8 seconds of an open-lid flush — completely invisible to the naked eye.
564K
Annual Deaths
WHO estimates unsafe sanitation facilities account for approximately 564,000 deaths annually worldwide.
36%
Norovirus Contamination
36% of environmental swabs in public restrooms tested positive for norovirus contamination.
89%
Bacterial Contamination
A study of 7,482 samples from public restrooms found 89.25% had detectable bacterial contamination.
2×
Surface Contamination
Open-lid flushing results in twice as many bacterial colonies on surrounding surfaces vs. closed-lid.
